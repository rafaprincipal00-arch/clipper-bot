Privacy Policy

Clipper Bot is a personal tool operated by its owner to publish short video clips to the owner's own social media accounts.

Data accessed. When the owner connects an account (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram), the tool receives an OAuth access token and basic profile information (account name and ID) needed to upload videos.

Use. Tokens are used only to upload videos the owner has produced and to check the upload status. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage. Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private CI configuration. They are never logged or displayed publicly.

Deletion. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's connected-apps settings; revoking deletes the tool's ability to post. Contact: rafaprincipal00@gmail.com.
